## Rabbasca underground
Rabbascan archives mention the existance of their warp-field stabilizers, powerful devices converting spacetime anomalies into a stable warp-field that makes rabbascan warp-tech possible. Locate stabilizers, join their journeys through the depths of various planets and gain access to even stronger warp technology.

This is a challenge/puzzle mod that expands [Rabbasca](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/planet-rabbasca), utilizing its warp technology to interact with a small planet-like surface constantly changing place, but always deep underground where no engineer can go. Building space is a limited resource that can only be expanded later. Warp your factory in using Rabbasca's warp pylons, but all material has to be sourced locally, as recyclers will not function here.

### Spacetime anomalies
The warp-field stabilizer is seeking anomalies, warping from one location to the next, purging them to stabilize the warpfield in the process. 
Anomalies manifest into other resources depending on the current location. This gives access to all the resources needed to establish a factory.

### Level up the stabilizer
By purging anomalies, the stabilizer gradually unlocks powerspikes, which can be used to craft upgrades, notably more anomaly harvesters, power cells, and building space expansions. The required purging costs scale with each level, so use them wisely.
Since there are many stabilizers in the universe, if you mess up you can abandon the current one and locate a new one.

### Learn from forgotten archives
Use a special new building to locate archives which contain rabbascan knowledge. If you can manage to decipher it, it may unlock powerful technologies. Or drive you insane. Handle with caution!

### Rewards
#### Warpotron
Like a spidertron, but it can fly and comes with a warpdrive preinstalled.
Call your warpotron fleet from anywhere, for immediate battle assistance in the whole galaxy.

#### Warp pylons
Pylons can now be crafted, so you no longer have to plunder rabbascan vaults to obtain them in battle.

#### VERY strong modules
Put powerspikes into modules to supercharge them. These modules are extremely strong, but due to the limited nature of powerspikes it is unlikely you will be mass-producing them.

#### Short-range remote access point
Perfect for situations where building space is thight, these chests connect to a nearby crafting machine to put items in or out.
