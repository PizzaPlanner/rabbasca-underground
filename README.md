## Rabbasca underground
Rabbascan archives mention the existance of their warp-field stabilizers, powerful devices converting spacetime anomalies into a stable warp-field that makes rabbascan warp-tech possible. Locate stabilizers, join their journeys through the depths of various planets and gain access to even stronger warp technology.

This is a challenge/puzzle mod that expands [Rabbasca](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/planet-rabbasca), utilizing its warp technology to interact with a small planet-like surface constantly changing place, but always deep underground where no engineer can go. Warp in your factory using Rabbasca's warp pylons, but material has to be sourced locally, as recyclers will not function here.

### Spacetime anomalies
The warp-field stabilizer is seeking this new resource, warping from one location to the next, purging anomalies to stabilize the warp-field in the process.
Anomalies generate other resources depending on the current location, requiring temporary mining outposts. 
Anomalies are limited but each new location will have new anomalies. Since the stabilizer requries them as fuel, and continuously harvests them, it can get stranded in one location forever. You will then have to abandon it and find a new one...
